# uuu: company engine

An app that runs a company end to end with people and AI agents. Planning stage: no code yet.

- `company-engine-blueprint.md`: the design, with a pick for every business function. Read before proposing or changing any part of the architecture.
- `company-engine-repos.md`: 368 candidate repositories by category. Read when choosing or replacing a tool.
