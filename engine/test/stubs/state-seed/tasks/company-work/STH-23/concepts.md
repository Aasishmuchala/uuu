# Concepts: Diwali offer
## 1. A new kitchen before the lamps are lit
angle: Order by the end of the month and the fitting is done before Diwali.
why: Most buyers want the work finished before guests arrive.
## 2. Visit the Whitefield studio, take home a free design plan
angle: A showroom visit in the festival weeks comes with a measured plan.
why: A visit is the step that most often turns into an order.
## 3. Five finishes, one festival price
angle: The five most asked-for finishes at one price for the season.
why: A short choice is easier to decide on than the full range.
## 4. From the workshop: how one island unit is made
angle: Photos of one unit from timber to fitting, in five steps.
why: Showing the work builds trust in the price.
## 5. Light up the corner you cook in
angle: Under-cabinet lights fitted free with any wall unit order.
why: A small extra that fits the festival and costs little.
