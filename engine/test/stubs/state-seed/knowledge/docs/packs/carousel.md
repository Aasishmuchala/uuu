# Carousel

The shape of a carousel post: what goes on each slide, in order.

## Parts, in order
1. The first slide: one short line that makes someone stop. At most eight words.
2. The middle slides: one idea each, a short heading and at most twenty words under it.
3. The last slide: what to do next, in one line.

## Sizes
- 1080 by 1350 pixels for a tall feed post, or 1080 by 1080 for a square one. All slides the same size.
- Between three and eight slides.

## What a good one has
- Large type that can be read on a phone.
- The brand's colours and typeface, and nothing from the can't-say list.

## What to avoid
- Long sentences, more than two typefaces, and pictures that are not the company's own.
