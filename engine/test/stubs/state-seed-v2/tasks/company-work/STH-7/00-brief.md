# Brief: Open a second showroom page on the website
## The goal in the owner's words
Open a second showroom page on the website for the new Whitefield studio, so customers in Bengaluru can see it and book a visit.
## What done looks like
A page that reads like the first showroom page, with the Whitefield address, opening hours, three photos and a booking line.
## Must-haves
- The same tone and layout as the Indiranagar showroom page.
- The address, opening hours (10:00 to 19:00, closed on Mondays) and a phone number.
- Three photo captions written for the page.
## Nice-to-haves
- A short line on the kitchen range that is on show.
## Anything unclear
None.
