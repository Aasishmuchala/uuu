# Brief: Plan the Diwali offer
## The goal in the owner's words
Plan the Diwali offer for the kitchen range and showroom visits: what we offer, to whom, and how we tell people.
## What done looks like
A one-page offer plan with the discount, the dates, the audience and the posts that announce it.
## Must-haves
- The offer runs only over the festival week.
- It does not take any kitchen below its cost price.
## Nice-to-haves
- A thank-you note for customers who bought in the last year.
## Anything unclear
- How much discount can the kitchen range take, in rupees or in percent?
- Is the offer for new customers only, or for repeat customers too?
