# Newsletter

A newsletter is a short email sent to people who asked to hear from the company. It gives
them something useful first and asks for something second.

## Parts, in this order

1. **Subject line**: under 50 characters, plain, says what is inside. No capital-letter
   shouting and no tricks.
2. **Opening line**: one sentence that says why this email is worth reading.
3. **The main piece**: one subject, in short paragraphs of two or three lines. A reader
   should be able to skim it in under two minutes.
4. **One more thing**: an optional short note, such as a tip or a customer question answered.
5. **The ask**: one action, with one place to do it.
6. **Sign-off**: as written on the Brand page, with a name.

## Length

- Between 150 and 400 words in all.
- Short paragraphs. A heading only if the piece has clear sections.
- One picture at most, with a short description for anyone who cannot see it.

## What a good one has

- A subject line that matches what is really inside.
- Something the reader can use today, even if they never buy.
- The same voice every time, from the Brand page.
- A reply invitation: people may write back, and someone reads it.

## What to avoid

- No more than one ask.
- No claim on the never-say list, and no promise the terms or support pages do not make.
- No prices or dates unless the owner supplied them for this issue.
- No long lists of links.
- No sending. A newsletter is a draft until the owner approves it and sends it.

## Before you hand it over

- Read it once more, start to finish, as the person who will receive it.
- Say plainly at the top if anything was unclear or missing.
- Mark every line that came from a guess, so the owner can check it.
