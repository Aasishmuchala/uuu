# Carousel

A carousel is a row of pictures a person swipes through, each with a few words. It teaches
or tells one thing and ends by asking for one action.

## Sizes

- Portrait slides are 1080 by 1350 pixels.
- Square slides are 1080 by 1080 pixels.
- Use one size for the whole carousel. Do not mix them.
- Keep the words away from the edges: leave about 90 pixels of clear space on every side.

## Shape

- Between five and ten slides.
- **First slide**: says one thing. One short line a stranger understands at a glance, and
  nothing else competing with it.
- **Middle slides**: one point each. One idea, one picture or shape, a few words.
- **Last slide**: the ask. One action only, such as "Message us to book a visit". It names
  where to do it.

## Words

- At most 40 words on a slide. Fewer is better.
- Big, plain type. A reader holding a phone must read it without zooming.
- Use the brand's colours and typeface from the Brand page, and nothing outside them.
- Sentences short enough to read in one breath.

## What a good one has

- A reader who stops after the first slide still learned something.
- Each slide could stand alone as a post.
- The order is the order a person would explain it out loud.
- Strong contrast between words and background.

## What to avoid

- No slide crowded with text. If it has more than 40 words, split it.
- No claim on the company's never-say list.
- No picture that is not ours or not allowed to be used.
- No second ask on the last slide.
- No prices or dates unless the owner supplied them for this carousel.
