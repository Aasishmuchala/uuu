# Title card

A title card is a short moving title: a few words that appear, hold and leave. It opens or
closes a video, or stands alone as a clip.

## What it is made of

- A background, one or two lines of text and, if wanted, the logo.
- The words move in simply: a fade, a slide or a gentle growth. One kind of movement.

## Limits, in plain words

- No longer than two minutes. Most title cards need about three to eight seconds.
- No bigger than full HD: 1920 by 1080 pixels at the most, with both sides even numbers.
- At most 30 frames every second.
- The finished file is a single video, not larger than 300 megabytes.
- The work stops by itself if it runs more than ten minutes.
- The computer must have plenty of free disk space or the work will not start.

## Shape

- A title of at most seven words. A second line, smaller, of at most twelve.
- Words stay on screen long enough to be read twice.
- Leave clear space around the words. Keep them inside a safe area, away from the edges.
- Use the brand's colours and typeface from the Brand page.

## What a good one has

- Text that is large and sharp, with strong contrast against the background.
- Movement that helps the reader and then gets out of the way.
- An end that holds still for a moment before it finishes.
- A still picture of the middle, so the owner can judge it without playing the video.

## What to avoid

- No flashing or very fast movement.
- No words that were not given for this card.
- No music or voices unless the owner supplied a file and the right to use it.
- No claim on the never-say list.

## Before you hand it over

- Read it once more, start to finish, as the person who will receive it.
- Say plainly at the top if anything was unclear or missing.
- Mark every line that came from a guess, so the owner can check it.
