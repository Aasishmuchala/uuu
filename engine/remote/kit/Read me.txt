Company Engine for Windows

This stick lets one Windows PC open the company engine that runs on your Mac.
The engine, its keys and its agents stay on the Mac. The PC only shows its screens.

What the stick holds: "Set up.cmd", this note, and a "files" folder with two
small PowerShell scripts, the Mac's public key, its name on your network and an icon.
Nothing is downloaded. No password is made or asked for.

1. On the PC, double-click "Set up". It shows a code and makes a "Company Engine"
   shortcut on the Desktop and in the Start menu.
2. Carry the stick back to the Mac. In Terminal, type: engine remote allow
   then a space, drag the file "seat-request.txt" from this stick into Terminal,
   press Return, check that the code matches the one the PC showed, and type yes.
3. On the PC, open "Company Engine". The Mac must be awake and on the same network.

The PC may read everything, make and answer tasks, write files and notes, and use
apps you switched on. Keys, Test, switches, sign-ins, limits, allowances and anything
with a price stay on the Mac: the PC says "This is done on the Mac."

To remove the PC: on the PC, delete the folder CompanyEngine in %LOCALAPPDATA% and
the two "Company Engine" shortcuts. On the Mac, run: engine remote remove <n>
(engine remote seats shows each PC's number).
