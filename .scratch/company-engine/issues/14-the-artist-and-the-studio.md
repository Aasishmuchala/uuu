# 14: The Artist and the Studio

**What to build:** A tenth agent, the Artist, makes sprites, animated GIFs, sprite sheets and simple vector images by script inside the fence, with its own tools. The Studio page shows CAD parts, images and animations, with a frame strip, play and loop, tags as named animations, onion skin, a palette and crisp zoom.
