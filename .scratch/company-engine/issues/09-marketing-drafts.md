# 09: Marketing drafts, with publishing switched off

**What to build:** The Marketer drafts posts and newsletters. Each draft appears in the owner's inbox and on a content calendar on the Marketing page. The owner approves or sends back. Nothing is published: the page says plainly that publishing is off until an account is connected, and there is no publishing code.

**Blocked by:** 05

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Plan tasks:** T13

- [ ] A draft the Marketer writes appears in the inbox and on the calendar with its title, kind and planned date
- [ ] The owner can read the full text, approve it, or send it back with a note
- [ ] A sent-back draft returns to the Marketer as a task with the note, and comes back as a draft once changed
- [ ] An approved draft cannot be changed afterwards
- [ ] A draft file that is written wrongly is shown as a problem, not silently dropped
- [ ] The Marketing page shows "Publishing is off" with how to turn it on later
- [ ] Nothing in this part can reach the internet
