# 05: The home server: inbox, goals, company and health

**What to build:** A small server on this Mac that the home screen talks to. It gathers everything waiting for the owner into one inbox, takes a goal in plain words and hands it to the Chief of staff, shows each goal's steps and result, lists the agents and what they are doing, and reports the engine's health. It is the only thing that talks to the board and the books on the owner's behalf.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Plan tasks:** T2, T8

- [ ] One inbox lists what needs the owner: approvals, questions, problems and finished goals
- [ ] Each inbox item says who raised it, when, what it is in plain words, and what the owner can do
- [ ] The owner can approve, send back with a note, answer, retry or dismiss, each with one request
- [ ] A goal typed in plain words becomes a task for the Chief of staff
- [ ] A goal shows its steps, who has each, the result and the Checker's verdict
- [ ] The company view lists every agent, what it is doing and whether it is healthy; an agent can be paused and resumed
- [ ] Health reports each part as on, off or unhealthy, when the fence proof last ran, what is not connected, and free disk
- [ ] Search finds goals by their words
- [ ] When the board or the books are off, the server still answers and says so plainly
- [ ] Every error is in plain words with what to do next
- [ ] It answers only on this Mac and refuses requests that come from another website
- [ ] Tests run against stand-ins for the board and the books, so no test touches the real company
