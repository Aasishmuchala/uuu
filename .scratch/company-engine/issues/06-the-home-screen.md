# 06: The home screen

**What to build:** One calm screen in the browser. It opens on the inbox. A box to give a goal is always within reach. There is a page for goals, the company, each department, knowledge and health, and a search box. It uses plain words, works in light and dark and in a narrow window, and says honestly when something is empty or not connected.

**Blocked by:** 05

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Plan tasks:** T9, T15

- [ ] Opening the address shows the inbox first, with a count of what is waiting
- [ ] Each inbox item can be approved or sent back in place, with a note when one is needed
- [ ] The goal box is on the inbox and one click or key away on every other page
- [ ] A goal's page shows its steps, the result, the Checker's verdict and the files
- [ ] The Company page shows every agent, what it is doing, and Pause or Resume
- [ ] Each department page shows figures, drafts waiting, what is in flight, and what is switched off
- [ ] The Knowledge page takes a document, lists documents and searches them
- [ ] The Health page says in plain words what is on, when the fence was last proved, and what is not connected
- [ ] An empty page says there is nothing to do; a part that is not installed or is off says that instead
- [ ] No ids, codes or tool names appear outside the technical details on Health
- [ ] It works in light and dark, and down to a phone-width window with no sideways scrolling
- [ ] Every control can be reached by keyboard and shows a clear focus ring
- [ ] It loads nothing from the internet
