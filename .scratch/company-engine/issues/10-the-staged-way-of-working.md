# 10: The staged way of working

**What to build:** Every role works the same way. The Chief of staff sizes each task. Small ones go to one agent and then the Checker. Larger ones get a brief, a plan, the work, a summary and a check, as documents with fixed headings. If anything is unclear the work waits and the owner is asked. A task can be started "plan first". After five failed checks the task stops and asks.
