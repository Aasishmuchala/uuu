# 12: Live trials: one real task for every department agent

**What to build:** Each department agent does one real task, given as a plain goal on the home screen, and the result reaches the owner's inbox. The owner's actions are tried for real: approve, send back. The home screen is checked in a browser with live data.

**Blocked by:** 06, 07, 08, 09, 10, 11

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Plan tasks:** T17, T18

- [ ] Bookkeeper: a draft invoice appears in the inbox; approving it makes it final in the books
- [ ] Sales: a lead is recorded and qualified, and shows on the Customers page
- [ ] Support: a reply is drafted for an open request; approving it sends nothing
- [ ] People: a staff record and a leave or attendance draft appear; the draft waits for the owner
- [ ] Marketer: posts and a newsletter appear on the calendar; one is approved, one is sent back and returns
- [ ] Drafter: a part comes back with the right size and volume and can be downloaded
- [ ] An agent answers a question from a company document the owner added
- [ ] At least one lesson written by an agent shows on the Knowledge page
- [ ] One draft sent back with a note reaches its agent as a new task
- [ ] Every page reads well with live data, wide and narrow, light and dark
- [ ] Trial records are removed from the books afterwards
- [ ] The proof still passes at the end
