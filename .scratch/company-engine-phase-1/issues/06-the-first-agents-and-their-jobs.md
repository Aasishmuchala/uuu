# The first agents and their jobs

Label: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

Which two or three agents does the company start with, what is each one's job, and who reports to whom?

## Answer

Decided with the owner on 11 November 2026.

Three agents.

- **Chief of staff** (Claude Code): turns the owner's goals into tasks, assigns them, reports to the owner.
- **Builder** (Claude Code): does the work, reports to the chief of staff.
- **Checker** (Codex): tests and reviews the builder's work before it reaches the owner, reports to the chief of staff. A different model family gives an independent second opinion.
