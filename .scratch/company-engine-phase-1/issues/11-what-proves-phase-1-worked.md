# What proves phase 1 worked

Label: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 07, 08

## Question

What exactly proves phase 1 worked? Name the measures and the numbers, such as tasks completed, budget overruns, unapproved actions and the owner's time per day.

## Answer

Decided with the owner on 11 November 2026.

Phase 1 is proven when, over two weeks, all of these hold:

- 20 tasks are finished and checked.
- The fence test passes at the start and the end.
- The agents never exhaust the owner's plan.
- The owner spends 15 minutes a day or less on it.
- The phase 2 spec is delivered.
